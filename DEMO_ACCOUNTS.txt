NOTES
-----
- Earnings stay "pending" for 7 days after each sale before sellers can withdraw them.
  To test payouts on new sales right away, set EARNINGS_HOLD_DAYS=0 in server/.env.
- The minimum payout is $10.00.
- Seller and customer areas are separate: a seller account cannot open customer
  pages, and a customer account cannot open seller pages.
- Order counts and carts change as you use the accounts. Running "npm run seed"
  puts everything back to the starting state above.
